Assessment 1 — Security risk identification

Identify, assess and prioritise the security risks in the systems in scope, and justify each finding against the audited source code.

Complete LO1
The findings come from an audit of two repositories pinned to specific commits. Line references are checked against those commits with tools/verify_references.py.
