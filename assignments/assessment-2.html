Assessment 2 — Critical analysis of development methodologies

Critically evaluate the security development methodologies, tools and alternative strategies available to a security engineer, and justify the choices made in this portfolio.

Complete LO2
The critical evaluation is grounded in the primary sources cited in the reference list, and each recommendation is traced back to a concrete finding in the threat model or risk register rather than stated in the abstract.
