Secure Software Development

MSc Computer Science · University of Essex Online · Module period October 2026

Assignments

This module is assessed through four assignments. Each assignment page groups its artefacts into four strands: Assessment, Collaboration, Professional Development, and Evidence.

Assessment 1 — Security risk identification

Identify, assess and prioritise security risks in the systems in scope, with a STRIDE threat model and prioritised risk register.

LO1 · Complete Open assignment →

Assessment 2 — Critical analysis of development methodologies

Critically evaluate secure development methodologies, tools and alternative strategies, and justify the choices made in this portfolio.

LO2 · Complete Open assignment →

Assessment 3 — Secure design and implementation

Apply identified risks to produce a secure design, implement controls, and demonstrate effectiveness with documented testing.

LO3 · Complete Open assignment →

Assessment 4 — Professional practice and development

Reflect on the work undertaken, record feedback received, and maintain a personal development plan.

LO4 · In progress Open assignment →
